--------------------------------------------------------
WICS One-Click Installation of nowRail and WICS Software
--------------------------------------------------------
1. If upgrading, make a copy of your configuration files
2. Disconnect the Board
3. Press and hold the right Boot button while connecting the USB-C cable
   The Microcontroller starts in a "Flash mode"
4. Open your WEB Browser and go to: https://realwireless.github.io/WICS-Install/
5. Select your "WICS Board" from the list of Boards
6. Click "Connect"
7. Follow the instructions on the Screen
8. When Done, Restart the Microcontroller (Left Button)
9. Upload your configuration file
10.If needed - Restart again
--------------------------------------------------------
You can use ESP Connect or WIFI to upload configuration
